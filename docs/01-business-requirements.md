# AI-Powered Loan Risk & Customer Intelligence Platform

## 1. Business Context

Financial institutions and NBFCs process large volumes of customer, loan, repayment and credit-related data across multiple systems.

Data engineers are responsible for building reliable pipelines that ingest, validate, transform and serve this data. At the same time, risk teams need timely insights into customer repayment behavior and potential default risk.

This project simulates an end-to-end cloud data platform that addresses both requirements.

## 2. Business Problem

The organization currently has customer and loan information coming from multiple data sources.

The business needs a centralized platform that can:

* Integrate customer, loan, repayment and credit data.
* Process data reliably at scale.
* Detect data-quality issues.
* Provide a 360-degree view of customers.
* Identify customers with elevated default risk.
* Explain the factors contributing to risk predictions.
* Allow business users to interact with the data using an AI-powered assistant.
* Monitor data pipelines and failures.

## 3. Project Objective

Build an end-to-end Azure-based data platform that combines:

* Data Engineering
* Data Warehousing / Lakehouse
* Data Quality
* Machine Learning
* Generative AI
* API development
* Business Intelligence
* Cloud deployment
* CI/CD

## 4. Key Business Questions

The platform should help answer:

1. Which customers have a high probability of loan default?
2. What factors contribute to a customer's risk classification?
3. What is the customer's complete loan and repayment history?
4. Which loan products have higher default rates?
5. How is repayment behavior changing over time?
6. Which data-quality rules are failing?
7. Which pipelines failed and why?
8. What actions may be appropriate based on the available customer and policy information?

## 5. Expected Business Outcomes

The completed platform should provide:

* Reliable and incremental data pipelines.
* Trusted analytical datasets.
* Customer 360 capabilities.
* Automated data-quality monitoring.
* ML-based loan default risk predictions.
* Explainable ML predictions.
* AI-powered natural-language interaction with customer and policy data.
* Operational pipeline monitoring.
* Business dashboards.

## 6. Important Scope Note

This is a portfolio project using synthetic data.

It does not use real customer financial information and is not intended for making actual lending or credit decisions.
